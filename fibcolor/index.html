Explanation

First, a sphere is placed inside the RGB cube tangent to all six sides. Then the specified number of colors are chosen at points along a spiral drawn on the surface of the sphere with the golden angle between consecutive points. This selection of points gives an approximation at evenly spacing the colors on the sphere. Next, the sphere is randomly rotated around its center. Finally, all of the currently selected colors are averaged with a single random color.
