# Content audit — Mathematics 7–12 (235) comprehensive rebuild

## Framework alignment

The bank retains all 21 competencies and the published six-domain weighting. Full fixed forms use the exact 100-question allocation represented by the published percentages.

## Visual reasoning

Visual interpretation is distributed across the bank rather than confined to statistics. Fixed full forms contain 38–40 visual questions. Depending on competency, students work from:

- coordinate and function graphs;
- complex-plane and transformation diagrams;
- sequence/function tables;
- unit-circle, trigonometric, and right-triangle figures;
- limit, derivative, velocity, and acceleration graphs;
- prisms and geometry diagrams;
- congruence and similarity markings;
- conics and vectors;
- histograms, box plots, bar charts, scatterplots, and residual plots;
- probability diagrams and trees;
- graphical/modeling representations used for mathematical reasoning and communication.

## Calculus breadth

The original calculus pool was expanded. The fixed forms are deliberately varied across calculus families, and the targeted competency quizzes contain still broader coverage. Topics include limits/continuity, tangent and secant rates, derivative graph analysis, second derivative concepts, FTC, FTC plus Chain Rule, Riemann/integral ideas, velocity, acceleration, optimization, related rates, work, center of mass, and calculus-based measurement.

## Difficulty

The rebuild intentionally errs on the demanding side. Each fixed form currently has 10 internally classified foundational items, 51 challenging items, and 39 advanced items. This is an authoring device used to prevent the fixed forms from being dominated by routine substitution questions; it is not a claim about official item difficulty.

## Repetition

Fixed full-test and fixed targeted-practice banks are globally separated by a signature consisting of the full prompt and choice set. Across the fixed materials there are 500 distinct full-form items and 1,008 distinct targeted-practice items, with no exact cross-bank overlap. Each fixed targeted quiz uses 12 different generator families.

## Notation

All mathematical notation uses the locally bundled MathJax. The final audit compiled 3,257 distinct TeX expressions with the same MathJax engine included in the package and found no parser errors or raw TeX-command leakage.

## Scope limitation

The practice bank is independently authored. Framework alignment and automated validation do not make it a psychometrically validated replica of the operational TExES exam.
