What changes: Each original page is rendered as a JPEG image and placed on a letter-size PDF page. This removes selectable text, links, fillable fields, annotations, and accessibility tags.

How it works

  • Portrait pages become 8.5 × 11 inches; landscape pages become 11 × 8.5 inches.
  • Content is scaled proportionally without cropping.
  • Image quality and resolution are reduced only as much as needed to meet the 10 MB limit.
  • Password-protected PDFs may prompt for the password.
Processing occurs locally in the browser. The page loads open-source JavaScript libraries from a content-delivery network.
