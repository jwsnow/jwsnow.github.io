# Answer and Convention Audit

Final audit completed August 8, 2026.

## Coverage

The bank contains 1,764 questions. Every question was included in one of two disjoint independent answer audits:

- **777 numerical questions** were independently recomputed from the exact values displayed to the student. These checks cover descriptive calculations, probability calculations, random-variable calculations, binomial and Poisson calculations, uniform/normal/CLT calculations, confidence intervals, hypothesis-test P-values, correlation, regression, chi-squared procedures, and ANOVA.
- **987 conceptual, interpretive, and setup questions** were independently checked against the relevant definitions, probability rules, confidence-interval interpretations, hypothesis conventions, P-value decision rules, and test-selection criteria.

Both audits finished with **0 unresolved answer mismatches**.

## Answer choices

- Every question has exactly four choices.
- Every answer index is valid.
- No question has repeated answer-choice text.
- A normalized-math comparison also found no numerically or formatting-equivalent duplicate choices.

## Course conventions checked

- For one-mean, two-independent-means, and matched-pairs inference, the site uses t procedures.
- For one- and two-proportion inference, the site uses z procedures.
- Hypothesis tests use the P-value method; no hypothesis-test critical-value method is included.
- In the one-/two-sample and matched-pairs hypothesis banks, \(H_0\) always uses an equality sign.
- Formal conclusions use **Support \(H_1\)** or **Do not support \(H_1\)** after the reject/fail-to-reject decision.
- Correlation hypotheses are stated in words: **There is not a linear correlation** versus **There is a linear correlation**.
- ANOVA hypotheses are stated in words: **All of the means are equal** versus **Not all of the means are equal**.

## Formatting validation

The bundled MathJax validator checked 12,185 mathematical expressions with 0 MathJax input errors and 0 raw-math formatting errors.
