Student progress sharing revision — August 11, 2026

Common student features added to Statistics, Texas Certification, TExES Mathematics 7–12, Real Analysis, and Grade 7 STAAR RLA:
- Student name required; Student ID explicitly optional.
- Student identity saved in browser localStorage and reused on later exports/reports.
- Export progress as a standardized JSON file.
- View/print a human-readable progress report with mastery and activity history.

Real Analysis progress management was expanded:
- Cumulative section mastery across section quizzes and mixed chapter/cumulative reviews.
- Question-type mastery for saved section quizzes.
- Activity history on the progress dashboard.
- Chapter/cumulative review results are now persisted and contribute to section mastery.

Instructor dashboard:
- Imports one or many student JSON files.
- Stores class data in localStorage.
- Keeps separate records by site/subject; Texas Certification subjects are not collapsed.
- Shows individual test/activity history within each site/subject record.
- Replaces an older same-student/same-site record when a newer export is imported.
- Allows student deletion and identity edit/merge.
- Shows class aggregate strengths and weaknesses.
- Exports a complete instructor backup as JSON.
- Restores backups by merge (newer records win) or complete replacement.
