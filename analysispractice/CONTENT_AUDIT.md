# Content audit notes

The entire 434-question bank was reread and reevaluated during this revision rather than applying only a global LaTeX substitution.

The audit used the uploaded *An Introduction to Real Analysis* notes as the content authority. Questions remain organized by Sections 1.2–6.5 and are intended to reinforce the definitions, theorem hypotheses and conclusions, proof ideas, calculations, examples/counterexamples, and section/chapter exercises in those notes.

Examples of substantive corrections made during the audit include:

- correcting the boundedness condition in the monotone convergence characterization;
- correcting statements about accumulation points and bounded infinite sets;
- correcting subsequence notation;
- correcting a Mean Value Theorem estimate;
- restating the epsilon-partition integrability condition as an if-and-only-if statement;
- repairing a malformed absolute-value/integrability item;
- restating term-by-term differentiation and integration of power series explicitly;
- replacing an incomplete Taylor-formula item with the full Lagrange-remainder statement used in the notes.

Notation was separated from prose throughout the bank. Ordinary English is no longer intentionally placed in MathJax math mode except through explicit `\\text{...}` commands when text is mathematically part of an expression.
